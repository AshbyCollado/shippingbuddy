# Research notes

This greenfield prototype uses the locked Next.js, React, Three/R3F, GSAP, Zustand, Zod, Vitest, fast-check, and Playwright baseline in `package.json`.

Packaging decisions are conservative prototype assumptions: all dimensions are millimetres internally; board allowances, tab geometry, score bands, cushioning presets, and RSC assembly allowances require physical cardboard calibration. ZIP codes are intentionally excluded from geometry.
